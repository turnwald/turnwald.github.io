## Research question

How does one decentralized safety-filter variant affect collision avoidance and liveness compared with a pairwise baseline?

## Minimum scope

Build an independent 2D simulation of 2–5 robots with one simple dynamics model, nominal goal seeking, a pairwise CBF-QP and one agreed variant, such as responsibility allocation. Define local neighbor and communication assumptions. No ROS, hardware or work from other projects is required.

## Evaluation

Compare nominal and filtered behavior in seeded crossing and bottleneck cases; report minimum distance, collisions, interventions, deadlock or goal completion, path efficiency and computation time. Discuss feasibility and liveness limits.

## Prerequisites

Python, control or constrained optimization and reproducible simulation.

## Expected deliverables

Simulator, baseline and one variant, documented interfaces and assumptions, seeded scenarios, plots and thesis report.

## First Iteration

**Objective:** Explore safety intervention and progress in a minimal multi-robot scenario.

**Task:**

Create a minimal multi-robot safety-filter prototype.

The first iteration may use 2D point robots or unicycle robots. Each robot should receive a nominal goal-seeking command, and a safety layer should modify commands when pairwise distance constraints become critical.

The first iteration should show:

- at least two robots with crossing or swapping goals,
- nominal behavior and filtered behavior,
- pairwise minimum-distance plots,
- intervention or active-constraint diagnostics,
- one liveness observation, for example whether the robots reach their goals or deadlock.

**Deliverables:** Provide the runnable simulation, trajectory and minimum-distance plots, intervention diagnostics, and a short observation about goal progress.

**Evaluation criteria:** Assess consistent nominal and filtered comparisons, interpretable distance and intervention results, and discussion of progress or deadlock.
