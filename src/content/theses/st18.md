## Research question

When does a learned uncertainty-aware margin improve the safety and conservativeness trade-off compared with a fixed margin?

## Minimum scope

Use an independent 2D simulated mobile robot, generated terrain/risk data, a nominal controller and a fixed-margin CBF or predictive safety filter. Train one uncertainty-aware margin model and use it to tighten the same safety constraint. No ROS, hardware, lab stack or outputs from other projects are required.

## Evaluation

Compare nominal, fixed-margin, learned-margin and reference-margin variants using safety violations, intervention, task success, path length and computation time on held-out conditions. Distinguish empirical findings from guarantees. No improvement over the baseline is a valid result.

## Prerequisites

Python, control or optimization, basic ML and uncertainty estimation.

## Expected deliverables

Simulation and dataset generation, baselines, margin model, reproducible experiments, interface documentation, plots and thesis report.

## First Iteration

**Objective:** Explore how a fixed and a data-driven safety margin affect a simple mobile robot.

**Task:**

Create a minimal Python prototype of a learned-margin safety filter for a simple 2D mobile robot.

The prototype may use a grid world or simple continuous 2D map. A nominal controller should move the robot toward a goal, while terrain or obstacle risk defines where extra safety margin is required. Implement a fixed-margin baseline and one learned or data-driven margin estimate.

The first iteration should show:

- a small map with obstacles and at least two terrain/risk classes,
- nominal and filtered trajectories,
- fixed-margin and learned-margin behavior,
- plots of margin, intervention, and minimum safety distance or risk,
- a short explanation of what is guaranteed and what is only empirical.

**Deliverables:** Provide the runnable prototype, a map and trajectory plots, margin and intervention plots, and a short discussion of guarantees and limitations.

**Evaluation criteria:** Assess the clarity of the fixed and data-driven margins, a consistent comparison, interpretable safety measurements, and separation of empirical observations from guarantees.
