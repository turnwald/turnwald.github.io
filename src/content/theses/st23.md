## Research question

How can grasp geometry, finger coordination and force control be combined for precise, gentle and reliable manipulation with an underactuated hand? How can realistically available feedback handle slip and contact uncertainty, and what tradeoffs arise between grasp stability, contact force and robustness?

## Minimum scope

Develop and experimentally evaluate methods for grasping and manipulating small rigid objects with the Inspire Robots RH56DFX hand in MuJoCo 4. Build on existing methods and provide a clear scientific contribution through model adaptation, control development or systematic experimental evaluation. Compare position control, fixed-force control and a contact-aware or adaptive strategy under consistent conditions. Document which signals are realistically available for control and distinguish them from simulation-only evaluation data. Tests with thin or deformable materials and real-hand validation are optional. Mobile navigation, whole-arm planning and camera integration are outside the scope.

## Evaluation

Evaluate grasp success, object slip or displacement, contact-force peaks and robustness under varying object properties. Use repeatable scenarios and a consistent comparison across strategies; document assumptions, limitations and the influence of sensing uncertainty. Discuss the tradeoffs between stability, force and robustness, including cases where a proposed strategy does not outperform a baseline.

## Prerequisites

Python, robotics and control fundamentals, and an interest in simulation and experimental evaluation. Familiarity with MuJoCo is helpful.

## Expected deliverables

A documented and reproducible simulation setup, implementations of the compared strategies, evaluation scripts and data, plots, and a thesis report explaining the contribution, results and limitations.

## First Iteration

**Objective:** Reproduce and evaluate a simple precision grasp with the RH56DFX in simulation.

**Task:** Use the publicly available RH56DFX MuJoCo model to grasp a small rigid object. Compare two basic finger-closing strategies under the same conditions, visualize the grasp, and report object displacement and contact forces. Document model and software versions and explain how to run the example. The exercise must run independently without access to laboratory repositories or a physical robot.

**Deliverables:** A runnable script with setup and run instructions, a grasp visualization, plots of object displacement and contact forces for both strategies, and a short interpretation.

**Evaluation criteria:** Assess reproducibility, a fair comparison, clear force and displacement reporting, and a reasoned interpretation of the results and simulation limitations.
