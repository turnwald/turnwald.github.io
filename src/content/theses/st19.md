## Research question

On what explicitly bounded domain can a learned barrier candidate satisfy or fail safety conditions, compared with an analytic baseline?

## Minimum scope

Use a self-contained point-robot simulation with a known safe set and input limits, an analytic CBF and a small neural candidate. Apply sampling, counterexample search and one agreed verification or bounding method; examine repair or retraining using found counterexamples. No robot or ROS is required.

## Evaluation

Compare analytic and learned candidates using condition violations, counterexamples, closed-loop behavior and computation. State the exact domain and assumptions. Failure to verify is a valid result; sampled success is not a proof.

## Prerequisites

Strong Python, control theory, neural networks and optimization or verification.

## Expected deliverables

Baseline and training scripts, falsification and verification experiments, visualizations, precise claim limits and thesis report.
