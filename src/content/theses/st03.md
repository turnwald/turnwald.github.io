## Research question

Can a short-horizon reference governor reduce safety violations while preserving task performance for a nominal reaching controller?

## Minimum scope

Build a self-contained Python simulation of a point-mass or kinematic reaching task, a nominal controller and a reference-level governor. Predict a clearly defined obstacle margin and modify unsafe references; specify model and tracking assumptions. No existing robot-specific pipeline, work from other projects, hardware or ROS is required.

## Evaluation

Compare nominal control, passive monitoring and the governor over reproducible scenarios; report minimum distance, violations, intervention, task error, completion and computation time. Clearly distinguish empirical behavior from guarantees.

## Prerequisites

Python, control or optimization, basic kinematics and simulation.

## Expected deliverables

Simulation, nominal and governor baselines, documented interface and assumptions, scripts, plots and thesis report.
